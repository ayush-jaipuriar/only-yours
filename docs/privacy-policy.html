Privacy Policy for Only Yours

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Welcome to Only Yours ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application (the "App") and backend services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when using the App:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, username, display name, and authentication tokens.
  • Gameplay & Couple Data: When you link with a partner, answer trivia questions, create custom question decks, or complete game sessions, we store your answers, scores, and couple progression metrics.
  • Device & Diagnostics Information: We may collect diagnostic data, crash logs, and device push notification tokens to ensure realtime notification delivery and app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the realtime two-player gameplay experience.
  • To authenticate your account and synchronize game sessions between you and your partner.
  • To send push notifications regarding game invitations and partner actions.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical glitches, crashes, and security vulnerabilities.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your answers, trivia guesses, and custom questions are shared only with your linked partner within the App. We do not share your private data with third parties except as required to provide core infrastructure (e.g. cloud hosting on Google Cloud Platform and transactional email delivery via Resend).

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS, TLS 1.3, and secure WebSockets (WSS), to encrypt all data transmitted between the App and our backend servers. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using BCrypt.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active. You can unlink from your partner or request complete deletion of your account and associated data directly in the App under Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us at support@onlyyours.app.
